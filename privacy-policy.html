1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the operator of IRIS Bot (“the Bot”) handles information when the Bot is used on Discord servers. The operator aims to collect only information reasonably needed to provide, maintain, and protect the Bot’s features.

2. Information the Bot May Process

Depending on which features a server administrator enables, the Bot may process:

  • Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, and related identifiers;
  • server membership events, such as join and leave timestamps, for join-and-leave detection;
  • moderation records, including warnings, cautions, timeout, kick, or ban records and the relevant timestamps;
  • message text and message or attachment identifiers or metadata to compare repeated messages for spam detection;
  • server configuration, such as enabled features, exception channels, and announcement-channel settings; and
  • technical information needed to diagnose errors, maintain security, and operate the Bot.

The Bot is not intended to collect passwords, payment-card details, or government identification numbers.

3. Purposes of Processing

Information may be processed to provide requested Bot features, detect repeated spam or membership patterns, maintain moderation history, deliver announcements, respond to support requests, troubleshoot errors, protect the Bot and servers from abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

4. Messages and Spam Detection

If spam detection is enabled, the Bot may inspect message content and relevant attachment information to determine whether the same or substantially similar content is being repeatedly posted. This processing is used for the configured moderation function. The Bot should not be treated as a perfect detector; false positives and missed detections are possible.

5. Sharing and Service Providers

The Bot operates through Discord and may rely on infrastructure or hosting providers selected by the operator to run the Bot and store its operational data. Information may be processed by those providers only as needed to host, secure, maintain, or support the service. The operator does not sell personal information.

Discord independently processes information under its own terms and privacy policy. The operator does not control Discord’s processing practices.

6. Retention

Information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Bot’s features, preserve relevant moderation history, maintain security, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Retention periods may differ by data type and server configuration. When information is no longer needed, it may be deleted or de-identified, subject to technical limits such as routine backups and legal requirements.

7. Security

The operator takes reasonable measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No online service or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Your Choices and Requests

Server administrators can disable optional Bot features or remove the Bot from their server. For questions or requests concerning information handled by the Bot, contact the operator at the email below and include the relevant Discord user ID and server ID where appropriate. The operator may need to verify a request and may retain information where required or permitted by law.

9. Children’s Privacy

The Bot is a general-purpose Discord utility and is not specifically directed to children. If you believe that information has been provided to the operator inappropriately, please contact the operator so the matter can be reviewed.

10. International Processing

Because Discord and infrastructure providers may operate in different countries, information may be processed outside your country of residence. Where required, the operator will take steps intended to ensure that such processing is permitted under applicable law.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Policy may be updated as the Bot’s features or practices change. The revised version will be published at the same location with an updated effective date.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact irisbot.support@gmail.com.
